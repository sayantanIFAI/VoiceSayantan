What this screen cannot show

  • The AI's own words. The agent does not write them to any file, so a call shows what the caller said and what the agent recorded, not the replies.
  • "Did you mean ...?" and "which one, A or B?" questions. They are not recorded, so clarifications here are the re-asks only.
  • Whether the AI offered a booking. It is not recorded, so "booking after an enquiry" counts calls where an enquiry came first and a booking followed.
  • A direct "that is wrong" from the caller. There is no detector; the panel counts visible disagreement (a correction at the read-back, a complaint, a request for a person).
  • The wait before a turn starts, the phone line, and the TTS queue: see the Speed section for what is and is not timed.
  • How wrong the speech recognition was. That needs a human transcript (tools/asr_eval.py).
